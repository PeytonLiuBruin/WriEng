WriEng bilingual core lexicon — sources, adaptations and limits
=============================================================

Edition: wn30-cow-cmu-wordfreq-2026-10-18-v1
Adapter: WriEng project, 2026-10-18.
Files: /data/core-lexicon.json and /data/core-lexicon-manifest.json.

This compilation selects 6,000 distinct English headwords with actual
English dictionary definitions, Chinese translation lemmas aligned to
the SAME WordNet synset, North American pronunciation phonemes, and
actual general-English wordfreq Zipf estimates. The original 172
WriEng teaching entries are a separate source group, not duplicated
to inflate the core count.

Adaptations:
  Select one evidenced WordNet sense per headword using the source
  sense-use counts with explicit reviewed exceptions. Join Chinese
  lemmas by exact synset. Remove WordNet adjective-position markers
  from display spelling and COW morphological plus-join notation.
  Omit individually recorded inaccurate Chinese source lemmas; no
  replacement translations are invented. Retain at most three Chinese
  lemmas per selected sense and at most two actual source examples.
  Select real CMU entries, correct explicitly reviewed heteronyms,
  preserve valid alternative source readings, and remove inline
  comments from phoneme strings. Exclude unresolved homographs,
  abbreviations, inflected forms and marked register-review items.
  Add unchanged numeric wordfreq estimates and exact ECDICT tag facts.
  Arrange the resulting compilation in JSON with source records.

Important scope limits:
  Chinese strings are source translation lemmas for a synset, not
  newly written full Chinese definitions or professionally certified
  translations. Source labels may be broad or imperfect.
  General Zipf values aggregate a spelling across senses and parts of
  speech in mixed text; they are not frequencies of the selected sense,
  US conversation, any specific exam, or a live 2026 corpus.
  Daily membership is an editorial general-learning selection.
  Exam membership only inherits ECDICT community reference tags. It
  does not assert a current official syllabus, count, frequency, rank
  or exam guarantee. Exam-frequency values remain unknown.
  All English examples shown in this core are actual source examples;
  missing examples, Chinese example translations, mnemonics,
  collocations and dictionary IPA are not fabricated.
  Structural checks and independent AI-assisted source spot checks
  do not constitute full professional linguistic review.
