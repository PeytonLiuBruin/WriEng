Changes: selected the literal headwords used in WriEng's seed modules;
queried wordfreq.zipf_frequency(term, 'en', wordlist='best'); arranged the
unmodified numeric outputs in an alphabetical JSON table with source metadata.
The application's display maps wordfreq's raw 0 missing-word sentinel to null;
the downloaded table retains the exact raw API output.
No exam, word-sense-specific or US-spoken frequencies were inferred.

English mixed-corpus frequencies combine multiple text sources. They are
general usage estimates for the fixed dataset shipped in wordfreq 3.1.1,
not current live measurements or an exam syllabus, rank, guarantee, or
measurement of American daily conversation. Higher Zipf values indicate
more frequent general use; they are logarithmic estimates, not percentages.
